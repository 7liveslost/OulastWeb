Programs & Modes

The different ways to experience Trials, from the core story path to competitive and limited time modes.

Program Genesis

Core Program · Path to Rebirth

The default core therapy path, made up of every main Trial and MK Challenge across four selectable difficulties: Introductory, Standard, Intensive, and Psychosurgery. Completing Trials here earns release tokens toward Rebirth.

Rebirth

Mansion

The final trial available once enough release tokens are earned through Program Genesis. Reagents who complete it are assigned a new identity and released from the Sinyala Facility, only for the cycle to begin again.

Escalation

Endurance Therapy

A rogue lite mode for 1 4 players. Reagents start with nothing, choosing Rig, prescription, amp, and item upgrades between an endless run of randomized Trials with rising difficulty. If the whole team dies, the run ends a major Trial appears every 10th stage.

Invasion

Reagent vs. Imposter

A permanent PvP mode where players can join a Trial as an Imposter a knife wielding invader disguised among the Reagent team. Most Specialist Ex Pop stop spawning while an Imposter is present, replaced by the human threat instead.

Trial Maker

Custom Trials

A creation toolkit that lets players build and share their own custom Trials using existing maps, variators, and objectives, including a rotation of community made Featured Trials.

Routine Therapy

Weekly rotation

Semi regular limited time programs built around specific Variators, refreshing on a rotation. Completing enough tasks earns a themed poster for the player's Sleep Room cell.

Limited Time Events

Seasonal

Larger scheduled events tied to seasonal updates such as Winter Kills, Prime Time, Program Geister, and the Chemical Leak event each bringing unique Variators, MK Challenges, and an Event Catalog of exclusive cosmetics while they run.

Fan made. Not affiliated with Red Barrels.
