Prime Assets

The five ruling antagonists of the Sinyala Facility. Each one patrols their own Trial Environment and can instantly down a Reagent who gets caught.

Leland Coyle

Police Station · Courthouse · Prison Farm

A corrupt ex police sergeant wielding an electrified baton. Blue sparks and crackling electricity give away his position in the dark, and his lit cigarette smoke can be spotted from a distance. A hit from his baton disables your Rig for 30 seconds.

Mother Gooseberry

Fun Park · Toy Factory

A disgraced children's TV host obsessed with her puppet, Doctor Futterman. She patrols carnival and toy factory settings and can inflict heavy bleeding damage with her drill armed puppet.

Franco Il Bambino Barbi

Downtown · The Docks · The Suburbs

A drug dealing gangster who rules the city district environments. He sets motion sensor traps that trigger when Reagents walk through them.

Otto and Arora Kress

Shopping Mall · Television Studio

Conjoined twins. Otto wields a loud chainsaw, while Arora sets poison gas Nausea Traps that hang from the ceiling. Best avoided in darkness, where their constant bickering gives away their position.

Liliya Bogomolova

The Resort

A masked, mantis like priestess who disguises herself as one of the many mannequins scattered through her alpine lodge. The X Ray and Jammer Rigs help you spot her.
