Police Station

Kill the Snitch

Main Trial · Leland Coyle

A prisoner is testifying against Murkoff, and the Reagents are sent in to silence him before he can talk.

  1. Enter the Police Station and make your way to the Security Room, following the signs posted around the front of the precinct. Loot any throwables and medicine along the way, but don't worry about antidotes yet.
  2. Search nearby police corpses to find keys marked with symbols matching the shutters blocking your path. Hover over a body to check if it holds the key you need before committing to search it.
  3. Unlock each shutter with its matching key. Coyle and roaming Ex Pop patrol this whole stretch, so move between cover rather than across open rooms.
  4. Once the path is clear, find the snitch and push his chair through the Back Offices toward the electric chair at the end of the route.
  5. With the snitch secured, activate the surrounding panels and hold them down to charge the electrocution. Multiple Reagents holding panels at once speeds it up Coyle will return during this phase, so be ready to break off and evade.
  6. Once the snitch is electrocuted, make your way back to the starting terminal to complete the Trial.

Sabotage the Lockdown

MK Challenge · Leland Coyle

The Police Station has gone into lockdown, and the Reagents must power a single generator buried deep in the Underground Parking to force the exit open.

  1. Enter from the Backalley and grab the gas canister and basement map sitting together near the stairs down this is the only canister on the map, so don't lose track of it.
  2. Carry the canister down into the Underground Parking and work your way through Sector A, Sector B, and Sector C toward the far end of the basement, where the generator is marked on the map.
  3. Coyle begins stalking the basement shortly after you go downstairs, and the layout of blocked doors and detours changes every run, so expect to backtrack even with the map's help.
  4. At the generator, pour in the gas, then complete the switch puzzle on both side panels until every light on the front runs green before pulling the ripcord to start it.
  5. The generator sits in a well lit spot, so work quickly powering it opens the metal gate beside it, which leads to the ledge back up to street level and the exit Shuttle.

Release the Prisoners

MK Challenge · Leland Coyle

Reagents start the Trial split into four separated Sections of the Police Station and must each find a key before they can regroup and break the Jail open.

  1. You'll spawn alone in one of four Sections (Patrol Department, Administrative Wing, and two others), each sealed off from the rest by Remote Control Gates in co op, every Reagent starts in a different Section.
  2. Search your Section for a key. The number of keys needed to unlock the Security Room scales with how many Reagents are in the Trial, so everyone needs to find one.
  3. Grunts, a Night Hunter, and at least one Big Grunt hunt through the dark while you search, and you're on your own until you find your key and open your Section's gate.
  4. Once you have your key, head down the hall toward the Security Room Coyle patrols this connecting hallway, so time your approach carefully.
  5. Insert your key into the Security Room's lock. Once every key is in, press the Button Switch inside to release the four prisoner mannequins from their cells and trigger Coyle's jailbreak announcement over the intercom.
  6. Leave through the newly unlocked Jail gate and make for the same shuttle used in Kill the Snitch to escape.

Teach the Police Officer

MK Challenge · Leland Coyle

Reagents must swap Coyle's police training film for one of Murkoff's own reels, hidden behind a chain of locked rooms scattered across the whole Police Station.

  1. Enter from the Attic and make your way down to the Auditorium, where a projector is looping Coyle's propaganda training film in front of rows of officer mannequins.
  2. Use the Key provided at the start to unlock the Audio Video room next to the Auditorium, which gives you two more Keys the chalkboard here diagrams how the room chain works.
  3. Each of those two Keys always unlocks a room containing two further Keys. From there, every remaining Key opens one more locked room, which will either dead end in hanging mannequin heads or contain the Film Reel you're notified with a sound cue if you open the right one.
  4. The Film Reel can turn up on any floor upper floors, ground floor, or basement so split up and check rooms as they unlock rather than committing to one route.
  5. Coyle and various Ex Pop patrol the entire building throughout, and several Proximity Gates will trigger and block routes if you get too close, so move carefully between rooms.
  6. Once you find the reel, carry it back to the Auditorium and swap it into the projector, then make your way out to complete the Trial.

Other known Police Station trials: Vindicate the Guilty, Eliminate the Past, Seize the Narcotics, and Cancel the Autopsy. Full walkthroughs coming soon.

Fun Park

Grind the Bad Apples

Main Trial · Mother Gooseberry

Mother Gooseberry welcomes the Reagents to her dental themed carnival before tasking them with feeding a boatload of "misbehaving" animatronic children into the park's central grinder.

  1. After Gooseberry's opening song and a grim little demonstration, make your way to the Diner in the middle of the park, where blood filled reservoirs hold the Hearts you'll need throughout the Trial.
  2. Throw Hearts at the carnival games around the park to win Tickets you need enough to redeem at the ticket booth before the Root Canal ride will open.
  3. Inside the Root Canal, push the duck boat of animatronic children forward. Gooseberry will cut the water supply partway through, so track down two to four pipe valves scattered around the ride to restore it.
  4. With the water back on, push the boat the rest of the way to the grinding area at the end of the ride.
  5. As the boat is fed into the grinder, Gooseberry bursts in through an Ex Pop Insertion Gate to try to save the children keep your distance and let the grinder finish its work.
  6. Once the boat is destroyed, backtrack out through the Fun Park to the exit Shuttle.

Punish the Miscreants

MK Challenge · Mother Gooseberry

A reuse of an early, scrapped idea for Grind the Bad Apples: instead of a boat ride, Reagents hunt down four animatronic children hidden around the park and feed them into the Barn's grinder by hand.

  1. Make your way into the Barn, where a grinder sits in the middle of the room this is your delivery point for the whole Trial.
  2. Locate the four child mannequins, one hidden in each of the park's colour coded zones (plus one in the Barn's own attic). Each is connected by a visible cable running back to a power switch.
  3. Follow a child's cable to its switch and press it to shut the mannequin down, then carry it back to the Barn you can't run while carrying one, and it will still make noise that draws attention.
  4. Feed each child into the grinder one at a time. Mother Gooseberry, Grunts, Pushers, Pouncers, and Screamers roam both the Barn and the Fairground throughout, so plan safe routes between zones.
  5. After the fourth child is ground up, return to the shuttle call button Gooseberry will spawn near the exit at this point, so bait her out of the way before making your final run.

Open the Gates

MK Challenge · Mother Gooseberry

With the park's exits electrified, Reagents must disconnect four Rolling Gates by tracing their wiring back to hidden power modules.

  1. Enter through the Barn Gooseberry is alerted to your presence the moment you arrive.
  2. Each of the four Rolling Gates (Barn, Castle, Diner, and Carousel areas) is wired to three power modules apiece. Follow the visible wires from each gate to find them.
  3. Disarm each module in turn no batteries are needed for anything else in this Trial, since disarming a module always yields one.
  4. The final gate, guarding the Carousel, is the hardest: the area is brightly lit and Gooseberry keeps a companion Ex Pop patrolling nearby, so track that patrol and move on their timing rather than trying to rush past them.
  5. Once all four gates are open, head to the exit Shuttle at the end of the Carousel area.

Drill the Futterman

MK Challenge · Mother Gooseberry

Reagents must fight through the Root Canal's flamethrower rigged ramps to reach Gooseberry's office and execute a masked victim, "Bootleg Futterman," with a power drill.

  1. Enter from the Root Canal entrance and split into Canal A or Canal B Shuttle Doors lock the choice in immediately, so your team may be forced to separate.
  2. Flamethrowers block the ramps up to the Root Canal's middle floor. Collect Hearts from the reservoir near the ramps and hit the Futterman Targets scattered through your canal to shut the flames off.
  3. With the targets cleared, descend into the Pump Room for your canal (A or B) and slip past the Night Hunter patrolling the maintenance area.
  4. Reach Mother Gooseberry's Office, where Bootleg Futterman waits chained in a chair, and press the Button Switch to drill him.
  5. Gooseberry immediately bursts in through an Ex Pop Insertion Gate flee back up through the Offices on the upper floor to the exit Shuttle.

Deface the Futtermans

MK Challenge · Mother Gooseberry

Reagents throw hearts at shooting gallery cutouts of Dr. Futterman across the park, racing a shrinking timer once Gooseberry seals the exit behind them.

  1. Start at the Carousel Area and grab Hearts from the two nearby reservoirs, then hit the four Futterman targets in the area to open the gate onward.
  2. In the Diner Area, find and disable a hidden battery switch before you can access more reservoirs and its own set of four targets.
  3. Continue clearing target sets through the remaining areas, including the Castle, while Gooseberry periodically enters to hunt and various Ex Pop patrol between rooms.
  4. Once you press the button to open the Barn's attic, Gooseberry seals the Barn's exit and declares "no one leaves mother" you now have 180 seconds to grab more hearts and hit the last three targets hidden in the attic.
  5. Hitting the final targets reopens the exit. Race back through the Maze to the Shuttle before the timer runs out.

Redeem your Freedom

MK Challenge · Mother Gooseberry

A ticket vendor in Gooseberry's Office offers freedom in exchange for tickets pulled from ten dispensers scattered through the Root Canal.

  1. Collect the first Ticket presented to you at the entrance, then track down the remaining nine Ticket Dispensers: five in the lower floor Canals, five more in the upper floor Offices.
  2. Each Reagent can only carry four Tickets at once, so plan for multiple trips back to the ticket booth rather than trying to gather everything in one run.
  3. Mother Gooseberry patrols the routes leading to the Office throughout, and additional hostile Ex Pop join in as soon as the first two Tickets are collected.
  4. Redeem all ten Tickets at the booth to earn your freedom, then make your way to the exit Shuttle.

Beguile the Children

MK Challenge · Mother Gooseberry

Reagents hijack Gooseberry's in park TV broadcast, feeding a corrupted subliminal message to a captive audience of child mannequins.

  1. Enter from the Diner Area and head through the Space Station attraction to reach the Illusion Castle.
  2. In the Illusion Castle's courtyard, gather four numbered Film Reels along with a projector and screen.
  3. Insert each Film Reel into the projector in order each one reveals one digit of a four symbol code while scratching out the others, so keep track of the sequence as you go.
  4. Bring the completed code to the auditorium with the Puzzle Boxes and enter it, then press the Button Switch to overwrite the children's TV program.
  5. With the subliminal message inserted, make your way to the exit Shuttle while Gooseberry and her Ex Pop close in.

Win the Truth

MK Challenge · Mother Gooseberry

A twisted game show hosted by a mannequin Trivia Host, where Reagents must search rooms for the right symbols while ignoring deliberately misleading decoys.

  1. Enter through the Cat Playground and find the game show tent, where a Victim wearing a Doctor Futterman mask is trapped inside a giant hourglass.
  2. Each round presents four Signs with symbols and numbers underneath the hourglass. Search the required number of rooms (two for one or two Reagents, four for three or four) to find the matching digits.
  3. Watch out for decoy Signs deliberately showing the wrong symbol in other rooms these can be ignored entirely.
  4. Enter the solved digits at the hourglass and press the Round Confirmation Button. A wrong answer discharges electricity through whoever pressed it, so double check before confirming.
  5. Clear all rounds to free the Victim and proceed to the exit Shuttle.

Toy Factory

Unlike an earlier draft of this page suggested, Fun Park and Toy Factory are actually separate Trial Environments both run by Mother Gooseberry, but with their own map and their own set of Trials.

Pervert the Futterman

Main Trial · Mother Gooseberry

The factory has descended into producing explicit "Dr. Futterman" merchandise, and Reagents are sent to destroy the source: a masked victim called The Deviant, pinned to the production line itself.

  1. Enter through the Factory Main Entrance and discover it's sealed by an Electronic Lock head down to the Basement to find the key instead.
  2. In the Basement, a man trapped inside a trash compactor claims to have the key. Free him (or otherwise deal with the compactor) to retrieve it.
  3. Unlock the factory's front gate and make your way to the factory floor, where The Deviant hangs from the production rail wearing a Dr. Futterman mask.
  4. Feed The Deviant through each of the factory's three Input Machines (Sections A, B, and C) in turn. Each requires its own Foreman Key, found in that section's upstairs Control Room, to unlock the Input Machine's Electric Lock.
  5. Each machine also needs raw materials fed into it before it will process The Deviant gather the materials for a section before triggering its machine.
  6. Once all three machines have run, make your way back out through the factory to the exit Shuttle.

The Toy Factory's known MK Challenges are Crush the Sex Toys and Incinerate the Sex Toys (pushing carts of contraband through the East and West Shipping Docks to a generator or incinerator, unlocking gates with Keys A D along the way), Shutdown the Factory and Fumigate the Factory (both routed through the Shipping Docks, the latter requiring valves found across the factory to spread toxic gas), and Flatten the Foreman (restoring power to a compactor to crush the Foreman before racing to the East Shipping Dock shuttle). Downtown

Story Trial

Main Trial · Franco Barbi

Reagents infiltrate Franco Barbi's city district, entering through a subway station connected to the street above.

  1. Start in the Subway entrance and make your way up through the ticket office and stairs to reach street level.
  2. Move carefully through Downtown's streets and storefronts Franco favors motion sensor traps that trigger when walked through, so approach unfamiliar doorways slowly.
  3. Work through the Trial's objective markers, staying alert for Franco's chainsaw sound and the erratic chatter of nearby Ex Pop.
  4. Complete the final objective and make your way to the extraction point to finish the Trial.

Downtown's known MK Challenges include Kidnap the Mistress (identifying and delivering the right prostitute mannequin to a police van) and Spread the Disease (spreading STD canisters across three randomized locations in the Streets or Sewers). Full walkthroughs coming soon.

Courthouse

Escape the Courthouse

MK Challenge · Leland Coyle

Reagents enter through the Jail, where a Scapegoat waits in the Waiting Room alongside Microwave Triggers used to progress the objective.

  1. Enter from the Jail into the Waiting Room, grab a Microwave Trigger from the stand, and cook the first Scapegoat twice to advance out of Section 1 into the Main Courtroom, Jury Room, and Backstage.
  2. In Section 2, you're given Trackers in the Courtyard. Hunt down and cook two more Scapegoats in this section to progress.
  3. Section 3 covers the Lobby and its adjacent rooms. Pass through a rig disabler here and stay well clear of the Berserker guarding the Lobby.
  4. A final Scapegoat hides in one of the two rooms adjoining the Lobby and must be cooked three times before the Courthouse's exit unlocks.
  5. With the last Scapegoat down, make your way to the exit Shuttle.

Destroy the Evidence

MK Challenge · Leland Coyle

Reagents must dissolve a body serving as "evidence" using acid buckets scattered across the Courthouse's ground floor.

  1. Enter from the Front Entrance and check the map next to the first acid bucket in the Lobby it marks three sections (Lobby, Library, Evidence Storage) where acid buckets can be found, and a red arrow pointing to the Main Courtroom.
  2. Carefully carry each acid bucket you find to the Main Courtroom without spilling it on yourself, avoiding Ex Pop along the way.
  3. Pour each bucket into the bath containing the evidence a dead Reagent while staying clear of Coyle, who patrols this area heavily.
  4. Once all the acid has been poured and the body is fully dissolved, cross the Timed Gates before they close and leave through the exit Shuttle.

Tilt the Scales of Justice

MK Challenge · Leland Coyle

Reagents must load severed heads onto Lady Justice's scales in the Lobby to outweigh a pile of money, using Blacklight puzzle codes solved against a ticking clock.

  1. Enter from the Back Entrance, where evidence a decapitated head is provided immediately.
  2. Carry each head upstairs, cross the Main Courtroom's upper balcony, and drop it onto the money side of Lady Justice's scales via the wooden plank in the Lobby to tip the balance.
  3. Additional heads require solving Blacklight puzzles for a four digit code within a 90 second window let the timer expire and an alarm sounds, drawing enemies and electrocuting anyone still in the room.
  4. Coyle patrols the entire Courthouse throughout, and Sound Detector Gates on the upper floor can temporarily seal routes if you're too loud, so move deliberately.
  5. Once enough heads outweigh the money, make your way to the exit Shuttle.

Fuel the Release

MK Challenge · Leland Coyle

Reagents must haul gas cans between three separate generators in the Main Courtroom, the Courtyard, and finally the Lobby to force the Courthouse's exit gate open.

  1. Enter from the Jail and grab the first gas can, carrying it to the generator in the Main Courtroom to open the next section's gate.
  2. Take the second gas can provided to the generator in the Courtyard your route there alternates randomly between the Evidence Storage and the Library.
  3. Coyle and various Ex Pop stalk you throughout both deliveries, so keep an eye out for cover along your route.
  4. With the second gate open, carry the final gas can back through whichever route you used (Library or Evidence Storage) to the last generator, sitting in the middle of the Lobby.
  5. Refuel and reactivate the final generator while avoiding Coyle's patrol to open the exit gate, then leave through the Shuttle.

Sentence the Prosecuted

MK Challenge · Leland Coyle

Reagents decode hidden symbols scattered through the Courthouse's wings to spell out a death sentence for a Reagent hanging above a meat grinder.

  1. Enter from the Back Entrance and pick up a Decoder, then find the first projection a triangle symbol imprinted on the stairs leading upstairs for your first code digit.
  2. Head down the red carpeted stairs to the Courtyard, where you'll find a Reagent hanging above a grinder next to four previously hung victims, along with several code screens.
  3. Entering the first code opens up the Office Wing and Judicial Wing, each containing more symbols needed to decode the remaining digits Coyle begins patrolling the Courtyard once the first code is entered.
  4. Watch for rig disablers randomly blocking either wing's entrance, and a Night Hunter that can lurk in the dark sections of both wings.
  5. Once all digits are decoded and entered, the screens reveal the hanging Reagent's charge. Press the primed Button Switch to drop them into the grinder, then leave through the exit Shuttle.

Bribe the Judges

MK Challenge · Leland Coyle

The Courthouse's newest MK Challenge has Reagents buying off corrupt judges with collected bribe money rather than working against the court.

  1. Search the Courthouse for Propaganda Posters and money briefcases scattered around the environment.
  2. Deliver an initial bribe to unlock access to the rest of the Courthouse's corruptible judges.
  3. Track down and bribe each of the remaining judges in turn while avoiding Coyle's patrol.
  4. Once every judge has been paid off, make your way to the exit Shuttle.

Full step by step detail for Bribe the Judges is still being confirmed since it's Coyle's newest MK Challenge check back as more footage comes in.

The Resort

Despoil the Auction

Main Trial · Liliya Bogomolova

Reagents infiltrate a private auction held at Liliya's alpine lodge, tasked with ruining the sale of a captive young woman known only as "the Lot."

  1. Enter from a Cavern into a manufactured blizzard the Resort's atmospheric conditioning has been rigged to freeze Reagents who linger outside too long, so keep moving toward shelter.
  2. Once inside, use Portable Blacklights to check the many Liliya alike mannequins ("False Idols") scattered throughout the Resort for hidden symbols marking them as carrying Jewels.
  3. Deposit collected Jewels at the Live Auction lounge to fund your way toward the Main Auction the Crypt beneath the Bear Section holds the highest concentration of Rubies but risks repeat ambushes from Liliya and a Hard Grunt on multiple trips.
  4. With enough funds raised, reach the Main Auction and replace the bidding paddles with four portable cranks.
  5. Operate all four cranks to tear the Lot apart limb by limb, ruining Liliya's auction, then make your way to the exit Shuttle.

Solve the Murder

MK Challenge · Liliya Bogomolova

A murder mystery MK Challenge: Reagents must deduce the killer, weapon, and crime scene from clues scattered around the Resort.

  1. Enter from the Spa and find a body in the Reception three Puzzle Boxes sit beside an investigator mannequin who asks for help solving the case.
  2. Explore the Resort to identify three pieces of evidence: the Killer, the Weapon, and the Crime Scene, while Liliya and various Ex Pop hunt you throughout.
  3. Shine your Portable Blacklight on blood trails and follow them to their end some are decoys that lead to the wrong room, so always follow a trail all the way through rather than assuming partway.
  4. The room where a genuine trail ends will display a random number on nearby TV screens; memorize it, since it forms the final digit of your solution code.
  5. Enter your solved answer for Killer, Weapon, and Crime Scene, then make your way to the exit Shuttle.

Incinerate the Relic

MK Challenge · Liliya Bogomolova

Reagents fund their way into the Resort's inner chambers to destroy a grotesque relic tied to Liliya's cult.

  1. Enter from the Crypt, where the sacrificial table has been replaced with a cauldron next to a Deposit Box, some Portable Blacklights, and a metal case holding the Bear Key.
  2. Collect $50 worth of Jewels from the Resort's False Idols using your Blacklight to unlock the Bear Key and access the Main Auction upstairs.
  3. In the Sauna and Spa Courtyards, power Faulty Generators to run Heaters and stave off the cold while continuing to collect Jewels Liliya and various Ex Pop hunt you throughout.
  4. The Wolf and Owl Keys you'll need are frozen in Ice Blocks; bring them to a Defrosting Plate at a working Heater to thaw them out (some plates are unusable, so choose carefully).
  5. Once you've deposited enough Jewels the exact total is randomized the final case opens to reveal Liliya's relic. Incinerate it in the cauldron and make your way to the exit Shuttle.

Prison Farm

The newest Trial Environment, introduced with Leland Coyle's return in Season 7: Project Boneyard. Coyle takes on a very different role here less a roaming Prime Asset and more the Prison Farm's actual master, keeping "the Warden" as his own personal captive.

Locksock the Warden

Main Trial · Leland Coyle

Reagents are tasked with overthrowing the Warden nominally the Prison Farm's ruler, but in reality kept as Coyle's own captive by inciting a full prison riot.

  1. Explore Cell Block A and its basement while Coyle patrols the cafeteria and steam valve areas nearby.
  2. Head to the Showers, where activating them startles the Warden and provokes a violent outburst from Coyle, who beats and expels him from the room.
  3. With the Administration Ward locked, incite a riot instead: turn up the steam flow to raise the temperature and ruin the prison's food supply.
  4. Once the riot breaks out, the door to the Warden's Office unlocks. Inside, the Warden is chained to a bed, watched over by mannequin prisoners.
  5. Bludgeon the Warden until he's disembowelled. The exit Shuttle arrives immediately afterward, giving you a three minute timer to escape the now rioting Prison Farm.
  6. Evade Coyle one last time near the Prison Entrance and board the Shuttle before time runs out, or you'll be shocked to death.

Prison Farm's MK Challenges are Bribe the Judges (shared with the Courthouse map see above) and Cancel the Broadcast, both introduced alongside the environment itself. The Biter, a fast Ex Pop with amputated arms, stalks all three Prison Farm activities. Full walkthrough for Cancel the Broadcast coming soon.

The Docks

Poison the Medicine

Main Trial · Franco Barbi

Reagents sabotage Franco Barbi's heroin refinery by poisoning his product at the source, all while trying not to wake "the baby" further.

  1. Enter from the Harbor Entrance and sneak past a Big Grunt pummelling another unlucky Reagent to death on your way to the Shipping Yard.
  2. In the Control Tower, press the Button Switch to lift a crane suspended tanker blocking your path this accidentally wakes Franco Barbi, who executes two hanging victims before storming off.
  3. Make your way into the Chem Co Laboratory, now converted into a drug refinery, and activate the drug cooker.
  4. Head deeper into the lab to a waiting Cart, unload two Drug Packages, and use the Trade Window to move them past Heavy Object Gates back to the cooker, pouring the heroin in.
  5. Head into the adjoining chemical storage room to retrieve a Poison Canister prolonged exposure to the chemicals or carrying the canister too long will start draining your health, so drop it and step into fresh air if your vision starts to change.
  6. Pour the poison into the cooker, then head to the Cargo Hold (activated via the nearby Control Room) to hide the finished drug packages inside bodies stored there.
  7. With the drugs hidden, make your way back out past Franco and roaming Ex Pop to the exit Shuttle.

The Docks' known MK Challenges include Cook the Informant (chasing down a relocating Scapegoat with faulty, single use Microwave Triggers), Empty the Vault, Poison the Cattle, Stash the Contraband, and Traffick the Product. Full walkthroughs coming soon.

The Suburbs

Liquidate the Union

Main Trial · Franco Barbi

Reagents cross a picket line outside Town Hall to execute the leader of a labor union striking against one of Murkoff's meat plants.

  1. Enter from the Scrapyard and head to the Town Square, where a riot is underway outside Town Hall led by mannequin workers from the Rare Horses Meat Plant.
  2. Push into Town Hall, where the Union Boss is held captive on stage while a recorded speech impersonating him plays to the crowd.
  3. Press the Button Switch to cut the speech as the curtains open, Franco Barbi reveals himself and frees the Union Boss into your custody, to be escorted through the neighborhood.
  4. Guide the Union Boss from house to house through the suburban streets, solving radio/frequency puzzles along the way as Franco and the Jaeger both threaten the escort.
  5. Deliver the Union Boss to the meat plant, where a workplace safety puzzle forces you to match injuries to the correct protective gear each correct answer brings him closer to drowning in the plant's waste.
  6. Once the puzzle is solved and the Union Boss meets his end, make your way to the exit Shuttle.

The Suburbs' known MK Challenges include Get Out the Vote (escorting a candidate house to house to collect votes and tickets), Eliminate the Legacy (matching thrown Hearts to Safety Sign symbols to open gates), and Disrupt the Neighborhood. Full walkthroughs coming soon.

Shopping Mall

Kill the Politician

Main Trial · Otto & Arora Kress

Reagents infiltrate a political rally staged by the Kress Twins' puppet candidate, "the Politician," and must buy their way in before turning on him.

  1. Enter the Shopping Mall as Otto and Arora watch from a window above the entrance, their puppet candidate's voice already playing over the mall's speakers.
  2. Track down Fuses scattered through the Mall's stores to unlock the sealed Department Store any store without a Fuse stays dark and inaccessible, and pulling a Fuse from its box seals that store and releases an Ex Pop into it.
  3. Inside the Department Store, spend the required amount of money by carrying boxes to the cashier cheap, light boxes are scattered everywhere, while pricier, heavier boxes wait in the back warehouse (which needs its own Fuse to open).
  4. Once you've spent enough, collect your ticket to the rally and make your way to the acid fountain, avoiding Otto and Arora as they patrol the west side of the mall.
  5. Refill and activate the acid fountain to burn the Politician and cut his recorded speech Otto and Arora appear above the fountain to vow revenge before dropping into the Main Plaza to hunt you directly.
  6. Flee through the Main Plaza to the parking lot and exit Shuttle, with the Kress Twins in pursuit the entire way.

Shopping Mall's known MK Challenges include Investigate the Minotaur (smuggling evidence of the Kress Twins' serial killer past to a journalist), Fabricate the Scandal (staging compromising photos around the mall), and Fund the Campaign (converting scanned box values into campaign tickets to lower the escape threat level). Full walkthroughs coming soon.

Television Studio

Silence the Idol

Main Trial · Otto & Arora Kress

Reagents break into a rain soaked television studio to sabotage the live taping of a talk show and permanently silence its host, "the Idol."

  1. Enter from a rain drenched Street, where a crowd of mannequins surrounds a limousine carrying the Idol celebrity talk show host "Bob Vanier" as he's wheeled toward the studio.
  2. With the front entrance sealed off, break into the studio's Reception from the side of the building.
  3. Work through each studio set's puzzle pushing objects like a modified stage light along marked rails and turning them to face the Idol correctly to sabotage the taping in progress.
  4. Locate hidden Murkoff agents planted in the audience of Studio 4 and swap their cheering cassettes for booing ones to tank the show's live approval rating.
  5. Once the approval rating hits zero, the talk show desk lifts to reveal a giant double bladed buzzsaw push it into the Idol to finish him off, then watch as a gagged mannequin's torso is drilled into his remains as a grim, "censored" replacement.
  6. Flee back out to the Street, where Otto and Arora make one last appearance, and leave through the exit Shuttle.

Television Studio's known MK Challenges include Cancel the Broadcast (shared with the Prison Farm map's season) and Change the Program. Full walkthroughs coming soon.
